1. Event Overview

Hacker Hunt is a cybersecurity competition where teams of ethical hackers will attempt to access a designated server using various cyber tools and techniques. The event is designed to test participants' problem-solving skills, knowledge of cybersecurity, and ability to perform under pressure.

2. Team Composition

  • Each team must consist of exactly 3 members.
  • Team members cannot be changed once the event starts.
  • Participants must register their teams before the event.

3. Event Structure

  • The event consists of one round lasting 3 hours.
  • Teams will be provided with server details and other necessary information at the beginning of the event.
  • Participants can use any legal cybersecurity tools and ethical hacking methods to access the server.
  • Teams must follow ethical hacking principles and should not attempt to disrupt the event environment or attack other teams.
